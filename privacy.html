Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who this covers

TheClipDept is a private tool used by its owner with their own TikTok account. It does not collect data from the public or from other TikTok users.

What is read from TikTok

  • Basic profile: display name and avatar.
  • Account statistics: followers, following, likes and video count.
  • The account's public videos and their view, like, comment and share counts.

How it is used

Only to show the account owner how their own videos are performing.

Where it is kept

On the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with advertisers or published. The login tokens TikTok issues are stored on that computer and are never shared.

Removing it

Disconnecting TikTok inside TheClipDept deletes the stored login tokens. Access can also be revoked from the app permissions in TikTok's settings. To have stored figures deleted, use the contact below.
